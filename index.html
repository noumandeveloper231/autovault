How it tracks every number

Every dollar tied to a VIN, a rep, or a filing.

Flip through the dashboard and you'll see the same data broken down four ways. Here's what each screen is actually doing.

Cost to the penny, per vehicle

Purchase price, auction & doc fees, repairs, add-ons, flooring, and days on the lot roll into one true net-profit and ROI number — so you know which cars made money and which went underwater.

Sales tax kept on file

The tax you collect is captured on every sale and tied to the VIN, then organized by your filing frequency with the next due date front and center. No more rebuilding it at quarter-end.

Payroll & commissions in one run

Salespeople on base plus commission, mechanics and lot staff hourly, managers on salary — all calculated automatically, with every payout logged and proof kept on file.

A live profit & loss statement

Revenue minus cost of goods, commissions, and overhead — with gross and net margin — shows exactly where the money goes and where you're losing it, updated as deals close.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by access controls and regular security reviews. Your dealership’s data is yours — export or delete it anytime.

Can my accountant log in?

Yes. A CPA / accountant login is included, so your accountant can file state taxes and review the numbers without you emailing spreadsheets.

What happens after my free month?

You’re billed $99.99/mo — no setup fees, no long-term contract. Cancel anytime and you won’t be charged again.

Do I need to install anything?

No. AutoVault runs in your browser and works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Add your vehicles and you’re running the same day.

Is there a per-vehicle or per-user charge?

No. Unlimited vehicles, VINs, and users are included — one flat price for your whole dealership.

Will it fit how my lot actually runs?

AutoVault was built with dealers for the real workflow — auctions, flooring, titles, commissions, and filings — not a generic CRM bolted onto cars.
